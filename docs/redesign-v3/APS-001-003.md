# Purpose

The Product Mission explains why AskChetna exists today.

Unlike the Product Vision, which describes the future, the Product Mission defines what the product should accomplish every day for every user.

Every feature added to AskChetna must contribute to this mission.

If a feature does not support the mission, it should not be developed.

---

# Mission Statement

Help people make better personal decisions through thoughtful AI-powered guidance presented in a calm, trustworthy and beautifully designed digital experience.

---

# Mission Objectives

---

## APS-MSN-001

### Reduce Decision Friction

Many users visit AskChetna because they feel uncertain.

The platform should reduce the effort required to:

- understand a problem
- organise thoughts
- evaluate options
- reflect before acting

AskChetna does not make decisions for users.

It helps users think more clearly.

---

Business Value

Improves trust.

Increases returning users.

---

Success Indicators

Users reach useful information quickly.

Lower abandonment rates.

Higher completion rates.

## APS-MSN-003

### Make AI Feel Human

Artificial Intelligence should never dominate the experience.

The platform should present AI as:

- thoughtful
- helpful
- transparent
- supportive

Avoid:

Artificial personality.

Fear tactics.

Authority claims.

Overconfidence.

False certainty.

---

Business Value

Higher confidence.

Lower AI anxiety.

Better user satisfaction.

# Mission Rules

The following rules apply across every platform.

---

MR-001

Always prioritise clarity.

---

MR-002

Always prioritise user trust.

---

MR-003

Never create unnecessary friction.

---

MR-004

Never overwhelm users.

---

MR-005

Every screen should have one primary objective.

---

MR-006

Every interaction should feel intentional.

---

MR-007

Every feature must solve a documented user problem.

---

MR-008

Every implementation must respect accessibility.

---

# Mission Success Metrics

The mission is considered successful when:

Users understand the product quickly.

Users successfully complete intended tasks.

Users return voluntarily.

Users recommend AskChetna.

Users trust platform recommendations.

Users experience consistent behaviour across Web, Android and iOS.

---

# Out Of Scope

The product mission explicitly excludes:

Medical diagnosis

Legal advice

Financial advice

Guaranteed predictions

Religious instruction

Political persuasion

Manipulative engagement patterns

Dark UX patterns

Clickbait experiences

Advertising-first experiences
